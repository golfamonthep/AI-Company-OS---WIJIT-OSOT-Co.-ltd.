# ไอเดียการออกแบบเว็บไซต์ AI Facebook Ads Guide

## บริบท

### Idea 1: Modern Performance Dashboard Aesthetic
**Probability: 0.08**

**Design Movement:** Data-Driven Modernism (inspired by contemporary SaaS dashboards like Stripe, Figma)

**Core Principles:**
- Information hierarchy through layered typography and subtle depth
- Minimalist color palette with strategic accent colors for CTAs
- Asymmetric grid layouts that guide the eye naturally
- Micro-interactions that provide immediate feedback

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Deep Navy Blue (#1a2a4a) for trust and professionalism
- Accent: Vibrant Teal (#00d9ff) for energy and innovation
- Supporting: Warm Grays (#f5f7fa, #e8ecf1) for breathing room
- Text: Charcoal (#2d3748) for readability without harshness
- Reasoning: The combination conveys technical sophistication while maintaining accessibility

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Hero section with asymmetric split: left side contains bold headline + CTA, right side features an animated chart or data visualization
- Content sections use a 3-column grid for course modules, breaking into 1-2 columns on mobile
- Sidebar navigation on desktop for course curriculum, collapsible drawer on mobile
- Floating action buttons for key CTAs (Sign Up, Contact)

**Signature Elements:**
1. Animated data visualization cards showing ROAS improvements
2. Gradient underlines on section headers (Navy to Teal)
3. Subtle glassmorphism effect on course cards

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states reveal additional details without page reload
- Smooth scroll-triggered animations as users explore content
- Loading states show animated progress bars instead of spinners
- Form inputs have floating labels with validation feedback

**Animation:**
- Section reveals: fade-in + subtle slide-up (200ms ease-out)
- Button hover: scale(1.02) + shadow expansion
- Card hover: lift effect (transform: translateY(-4px)) with shadow deepening
- Scroll animations: parallax on hero background, counter animations for statistics
- All animations respect prefers-reduced-motion

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Poppins" Bold (700) for headlines - modern, geometric feel
- Heading: "Poppins" SemiBold (600) for subheadings
- Body: "Inter" Regular (400) for readability
- Accent: "Poppins" Medium (500) for labels and CTAs
- Hierarchy: 48px → 32px → 24px → 16px → 14px

### Idea 2: Educational Storytelling with Warm Accents
**Probability: 0.07**

**Design Movement:** Contemporary Educational Design (inspired by platforms like Skillshare, Masterclass)

**Core Principles:**
- Narrative-driven layout that guides users through a learning journey
- Warm, inviting color palette that reduces cognitive load
- Hand-drawn or illustrated elements to humanize technical content
- Progressive disclosure of information (basic → advanced)

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Warm Orange (#ff6b35) for energy and approachability
- Secondary: Soft Sage Green (#6b9d83) for balance and growth
- Background: Cream (#faf8f3) for comfort and reduced eye strain
- Accent: Deep Plum (#4a3f5c) for depth
- Text: Charcoal (#2d3748)
- Reasoning: Warm tones create psychological safety for learners; sage green symbolizes growth

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width hero with illustrated background showing a journey metaphor
- Content flows vertically with alternating left-right text/image layouts
- Course modules presented as "chapters" with progress indicators
- Testimonials and case studies embedded naturally within learning sections

**Signature Elements:**
1. Illustrated icons for each course topic (AI, targeting, optimization, etc.)
2. Handwritten-style callout boxes for key insights
3. Progress badges and achievement indicators

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Smooth scroll reveals with staggered animations
- Interactive course cards that expand on hover to show more details
- Testimonial carousel with auto-advance and manual controls
- Form fields with encouraging, contextual help text

**Animation:**
- Hero illustration: subtle parallax movement on scroll
- Course cards: gentle rotation + scale on hover
- Icons: fade-in + scale animation as they enter viewport
- Progress bars: animated fill from 0% to target value
- Testimonials: fade transition between slides (400ms ease-in-out)

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Playfair Display" Bold (700) for main headlines - elegant, distinctive
- Heading: "Poppins" SemiBold (600) for subheadings
- Body: "Lato" Regular (400) for comfortable reading
- Accent: "Poppins" Medium (500) for CTAs and labels
- Hierarchy: 52px → 36px → 28px → 18px → 16px

</response>

### Idea 3: Bold Contrasts with Kinetic Energy
**Probability: 0.06**

**Design Movement:** High-Energy Digital Marketing Aesthetic (inspired by growth hacking platforms, crypto/fintech)

**Core Principles:**
- High contrast between dark and light elements for visual impact
- Kinetic typography and animated elements throughout
- Asymmetric, dynamic layouts that feel unconventional
- Bold, saturated colors that demand attention

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Electric Purple (#7c3aed) for innovation and power
- Secondary: Bright Lime (#84cc16) for energy and growth
- Dark Background: Charcoal (#0f172a) for contrast
- Accent: Hot Pink (#ec4899) for urgency and CTAs
- Text: Off-white (#f8fafc)
- Reasoning: High saturation creates excitement; dark background reduces eye strain for long reading sessions

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Hero section: full-screen video background with bold text overlay
- Diagonal cuts and angled sections to break monotony
- Floating elements and layered compositions
- Staggered grid for course modules with varied card sizes

**Signature Elements:**
1. Animated gradient backgrounds that shift on scroll
2. Kinetic typography with letter-by-letter animations
3. Glowing neon-style borders on interactive elements

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Aggressive hover states with color shifts and scale transforms
- Scroll-triggered animations that feel responsive and immediate
- Interactive elements have glowing effects on focus
- Form inputs with animated underlines and validation states

**Animation:**
- Hero text: staggered letter-by-letter fade-in (50ms per letter)
- Gradient backgrounds: continuous subtle color shift animation
- Course cards: 3D flip effect on hover (using CSS perspective)
- CTAs: pulse animation on load, scale + glow on hover
- Scroll animations: elements slide in from edges with momentum

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Space Grotesk" Bold (700) for headlines - futuristic, geometric
- Heading: "Space Grotesk" SemiBold (600) for subheadings
- Body: "Inter" Regular (400) for clarity
- Accent: "Space Grotesk" Medium (500) for emphasis
- Hierarchy: 56px → 40px → 32px → 20px → 16px

</response>
