# Company Memory

## Company Rules

- AI Company OS outputs are draft-only until a human approves external publishing.
- Product, health, financial, and performance claims require verified evidence.
- Agents should state assumptions when product facts are missing.

## Customer Insight

- Thai customers respond better to practical, clear, low-pressure explanations than exaggerated hype.
- Parent and family audiences value reassurance, safety, clarity, and simple decision criteria.

## Product Knowledge Pattern

- When product details are incomplete, content should explain the selection checklist instead of inventing benefits.
