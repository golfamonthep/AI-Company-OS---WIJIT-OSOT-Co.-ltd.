# Operations Policy

## Purpose

The Autonomous Operations Layer lets AI Company OS monitor, recommend, schedule, and prepare work proactively.

Operations are supervised. The system may prepare and recommend actions, but high-impact actions require governance approval.

## Allowed Autonomous Operations

- Monitor tasks, workflows, KPIs, memory quality, and agent activity.
- Detect risks and opportunities.
- Prepare draft workflows and reports.
- Schedule low-risk internal review tasks.
- Escalate blocked or risky issues.
- Recommend campaigns, research, workflow optimization, or SOP improvement.

## Approval Required

Human or governance approval is required before:

- publishing content
- spending ad budget
- modifying financial records
- deleting or archiving memory
- changing governance rules
- modifying permissions
- executing external irreversible actions

## Audit Requirement

All operations events must be logged:

- schedules
- triggers
- recommendations
- risk assessments
- approval routing
- failures
- generated reports
