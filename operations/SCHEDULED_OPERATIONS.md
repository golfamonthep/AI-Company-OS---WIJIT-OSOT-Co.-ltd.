# Scheduled Operations

## Daily Content Planning

- cadence: daily
- owner_agent: marketing
- workflow: content-production
- approval_required_for: publishing

## Weekly AI Company Review

- cadence: weekly
- owner_agent: ceo
- workflow: weekly-company-review
- approval_required_for: final report and approved learnings

## Monthly Financial Review

- cadence: monthly
- owner_agent: cfo
- workflow: weekly-business-review
- approval_required_for: budget or financial record changes

## Scheduling Rule

Schedules create internal review work only. They do not execute external actions without approval.
