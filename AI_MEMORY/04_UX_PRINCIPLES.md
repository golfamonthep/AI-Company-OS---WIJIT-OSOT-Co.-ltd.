# UX Principles

## Primary Experience

- CEO AI is the main surface.
- Internal agents should feel like CEO AI's team, not separate software the user must operate.
- The dashboard should answer: what is happening, what needs my decision, and what should I do next?

## Thai Business Owner Fit

- Use Thai-first labels and plain business wording.
- Avoid developer-demo language in primary flows.
- Translate backend states into human-readable business states.
- Make approval and risk clear without sounding technical.

## Visual Direction

- Light mode first.
- White cards, neutral background, soft borders, calm accent colors.
- Professional SaaS/admin feel.
- Avoid cyberpunk, neon, dark glass, and overly futuristic styling on primary MVP routes.

## Interaction Direction

- Prefer one clear CEO command/briefing surface over many equal panels.
- Keep approval queue easy to find.
- Keep workflow state visible at a glance.
- Show internal agent detail only when useful for trust, review, or debugging.

## Current Boundary

Do not redesign the dashboard yet. First preserve the current implementation and improve orientation around CEO AI in small phases.
