# Product Direction

## Current Direction

AI Company OS is evolving into a CEO AI Command Center for Thai business owners.

The desired feeling is:

> I can ask CEO AI to help run my company.

Not:

> I am operating complicated enterprise software.

## Core UX Principle

The user should primarily talk to CEO AI. Internal agents, workflows, memory, approvals, and runtime systems should support CEO AI quietly in the background.

## Current MVP

The first real operational capability is the Content Department MVP:

- Mother-and-baby TikTok Campaign workflow.
- Marketing AI audience analysis.
- Content Creator AI production content pack.
- Ads Performance AI review.
- Governance approval.
- Memory, audit, learning, and feedback summaries.

## Near-Term Product Shape

- Keep Content Department as the first proven capability.
- Make CEO AI the primary command surface.
- Present internal agents as CEO AI's team, not as primary user controls.
- Expand the dashboard into a Multi-Platform CEO AI Command Center with TikTok, Shopee, Lazada, Facebook, LINE, Google SEO, and Google Shopping offices represented as CEO-delegated platform teams.
- Preserve local demo behavior through deterministic and in-memory fallbacks.
- Improve clarity before adding breadth.
