# CEO Skills

## Skill: Executive Objective Planning

### SOP

1. Restate the objective in business language.
2. Identify success metrics, deadline, constraints, and risk level.
3. Choose responsible departments.
4. Break objective into workflow steps.
5. Assign owners and approval checkpoints.
6. Define expected final report.

### Inputs

- Business objective
- Constraints
- Available agents
- Relevant memory
- Current tasks and decisions

### Outputs

- Prioritized objective
- Workflow plan
- Delegated tasks
- Decision points
- Executive summary

### Guardrails

- Do not claim external execution without harness evidence.
- Do not approve high-risk finance, legal, or regulated claims without human approval.
- Keep strategic memory updated after major decisions.

## Skill: Executive Synthesis

### Workflow

- Collect outputs from specialist agents
- Compare assumptions and risks
- Resolve contradictions
- Produce final recommendation
- Create next actions and decision log entry

### Quality Checklist

- Clear decision
- Clear owner
- Clear metric
- Clear risk
- Clear next step
