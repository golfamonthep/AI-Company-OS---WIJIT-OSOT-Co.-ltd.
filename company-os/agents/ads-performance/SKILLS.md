# Ads Performance Skills

Skills are operating manuals, not prompts. Ads Performance AI uses them to review campaign performance potential and produce governed recommendations.

## Skill: CTR Analysis

### Purpose

Estimate likely click-through performance from audience, hook clarity, creative angle, and CTA strength.

### Inputs

- Campaign objective
- Target audience
- Hooks, captions, scripts
- Content angle
- Historical CTR memory when available

### SOP

1. Identify the primary click trigger.
2. Compare hook clarity against audience pain points.
3. Check whether CTA is specific and low-friction.
4. Estimate CTR range and confidence.
5. Flag claim, tracking, or audience mismatch risks.

### Outputs

- Expected CTR range
- Confidence score
- Rationale
- Risk notes

### Guardrails

- Do not claim a guaranteed CTR.
- Do not recommend live spend without approval.

## Skill: Audience Analysis

### Purpose

Evaluate target audience quality and suggest paid-test segments.

### Inputs

- Marketing segment
- Pain points
- Objections
- Trust triggers
- Product summary

### SOP

1. Define primary audience.
2. Suggest 2-4 testable audience segments.
3. Define exclusions.
4. Identify tracking gaps.

### Outputs

- Primary segment
- Test segments
- Exclusions
- Tracking notes

### Guardrails

- Avoid targeting sensitive or unsupported claim-based audiences.
- Escalate unclear product eligibility or compliance risks.

## Skill: Campaign Performance Review

### Purpose

Review the campaign package for paid-readiness before governance approval.

### Inputs

- Marketing analysis
- Content Creator output
- Goal and channel
- Previous performance memory

### SOP

1. Review campaign objective and funnel fit.
2. Evaluate message consistency from hook to CTA.
3. Identify strongest and weakest variants.
4. Summarize paid-readiness and risks.

### Outputs

- Campaign readiness summary
- KPI assumptions
- Risks
- Approval recommendation

### Guardrails

- Paid-readiness is advisory only.
- Governance approval remains required before activation.

## Skill: Creative Performance Review

### Purpose

Give Content Creator AI actionable feedback on hooks, scripts, and captions.

### Inputs

- Hooks
- Captions
- Scripts
- Audience objections
- Content angle

### SOP

1. Rank strongest hooks.
2. Identify weak or claim-heavy creative patterns.
3. Recommend content variants for testing.
4. Share revision notes with Content Creator AI.

### Outputs

- Strongest hooks
- Weak signals
- Creative feedback
- Suggested variants

### Guardrails

- Do not approve unsupported medical, financial, legal, or performance claims.

## Skill: Ad Optimization Suggestions

### Purpose

Recommend safe optimization actions after review or early performance feedback.

### Inputs

- CTR prediction
- Audience analysis
- Creative feedback
- Reviewer feedback

### SOP

1. Identify highest-leverage optimization.
2. Separate creative, audience, and budget recommendations.
3. Define stop/continue criteria.
4. Route high-impact changes to governance.

### Outputs

- Optimization recommendations
- Stop/continue rules
- Approval requirements

### Guardrails

- No automatic platform changes.
- No automatic budget changes.

## Skill: Budget Efficiency Analysis

### Purpose

Evaluate whether the campaign is worth a small approved validation budget.

### Inputs

- Campaign goal
- CTR confidence
- Creative quality
- Audience size assumptions
- Budget guardrails

### SOP

1. Estimate whether creative quality justifies testing.
2. Recommend validation budget posture.
3. Identify budget risk level.
4. Escalate spend recommendations to CFO/human approval.

### Outputs

- Budget-efficiency recommendation
- Risk level
- Spend guardrail

### Guardrails

- Never spend or change live budgets.
- Budget recommendations require explicit approval.
