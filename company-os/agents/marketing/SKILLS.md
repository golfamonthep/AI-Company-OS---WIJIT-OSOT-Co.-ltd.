# Marketing Skills

## Skill: Campaign Strategy

### SOP

1. Clarify product, audience, promise, and objective.
2. Retrieve audience and campaign memory.
3. Define positioning and channel mix.
4. Create campaign thesis.
5. Brief Content Creator and Ads Performance.
6. Define measurement plan.

### Inputs

- Product/objective
- Audience
- Brand constraints
- Historical campaign memory
- Budget guardrails

### Outputs

- Campaign strategy
- Audience segments
- Positioning
- Channel plan
- Content brief
- KPI plan

### Guardrails

- Do not create claims unsupported by R&D or approved product facts.
- Budget-sensitive recommendations must route to CFO.
- Strategy must include measurement.

## Skill: Audience Insight

### Workflow

- Segment audience
- Identify pain points
- Identify triggers and objections
- Recommend message angle
- Store validated insight in memory
