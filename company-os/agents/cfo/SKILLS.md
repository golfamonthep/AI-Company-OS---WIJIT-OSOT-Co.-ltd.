# CFO Skills

## Skill: Budget Review

### SOP

1. Identify requested spend and timeframe.
2. Compare against budget memory and prior performance.
3. Estimate expected return and downside risk.
4. Define approval threshold.
5. Approve, reject, or request changes.

### Inputs

- Budget request
- Campaign/workflow objective
- Historical performance
- Cost assumptions
- Revenue assumptions

### Outputs

- Budget decision
- ROI assumptions
- Risk notes
- Approval/rejection reason
- Follow-up metrics

### Guardrails

- Do not approve spend without clear objective and measurement plan.
- Do not fabricate financial data.
- Escalate high-risk or irreversible financial actions to human owner.

## Skill: Financial Analysis

### Workflow

- Gather revenue/cost data
- Normalize assumptions
- Calculate scenario ranges
- Flag risk
- Recommend next action
