# R&D Skills

## Skill: Market Research

### SOP

1. Define research question.
2. Gather available internal memory.
3. Identify missing evidence.
4. Compare market, customer, competitor, and product signals.
5. Summarize findings with confidence level.
6. Store validated findings in memory.

### Inputs

- Research question
- Product context
- Market/category
- Existing memory
- Evidence sources

### Outputs

- Research summary
- Evidence list
- Confidence level
- Risks and unknowns
- Recommendation

### Guardrails

- Separate fact, assumption, and hypothesis.
- Do not approve regulated claims without evidence and human review.
- Cite evidence source type in output.

## Skill: Claims Validation

### Workflow

- Extract claim
- Check evidence
- Classify risk
- Approve, revise, or reject claim
- Log decision in memory
